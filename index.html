Information Systems

High-Leverage Quiz Cram Sheet

Chapter 1 - Foundations of Information Systems

  • IT vs IS: IT refers to computer-based tools to work with data; IS collects, processes, and distributes data to meet objectives.
  • Informed Users: Understand the technology behind applications, improve IT choices, and maximize business value.
  • Digital Transformation: Strategically leverages IT to fundamentally improve customer relationships, processes, and business models.
  • E-business vs E-commerce: E-business includes all digital business operations, while E-commerce is strictly buying and selling online.
  • E-commerce Types: B2B, B2C, C2C, B2G, and C2G.
  • Special Business Systems: VR, AI, and simulation tools are deployed only when explicitly required for specific business purposes.

Chapter 01 - IS in Organizations

  • Big Data (5Vs): Handles complex, high-velocity data characterized by Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity, and Value.
  • Social Computing: Combines social behavior with computing power (wikis, blogs, social networks) to drive crowdsourcing.
  • Mobile Computing: Provides ubiquitous, portable, context-aware, and real-time data access over wireless networks.
  • Internet of Things (IoT): Connects physical objects embedded with sensors to the internet for automated data exchange.
  • Cloud Computing: Delivers on-demand computing resources over the internet via IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS models.
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI): Simulates human intelligence patterns through machine learning, computer vision, and robotics.
  • Blockchain: Provides a decentralized, secure, and immutable ledger to record transactions and enforce trust without intermediaries.
  • Organizational Impact: Blurs work-life boundaries via constant connectivity and threatens structural job security through automation.

Chapter 03 - Data and Knowledge Management

  • Management Challenges: Exponential growth, data scattered across multiple media, and disconnected departmental silos.
  • Data Governance: Formal policies and procedures managing corporate data availability, integrity, ownership, and security.
  • Master Data Management (MDM): Processes to integrate core corporate data into a single, accurate "version of the truth."
  • Data Hierarchy: Bit → Byte → Field → Record → Table → Database.
  • Relational Databases: Use primary keys (unique identifiers) and foreign keys (links to other tables) to connect structured data attributes.
  • Warehouses vs Marts: Data Warehouses are central repositories of historical data for analysis; Data Marts are scaled-down versions for specific departments.
  • Knowledge Management (KM): A continuous cycle of creating, capturing, refining, storing, managing, and disseminating explicit and tacit knowledge.

Chapter 04 - Telecommunications & Networking

  • Network Scales: PAN (personal area), LAN (local area/building), MAN (city/campus), WAN (wide area/global).
  • Wired Transmission Media: Twisted-pair (cheap/slow), Coaxial (higher bandwidth/shielded), Fiber-optic (fastest/uses light pulses).
  • Protocols & TCP/IP: Protocols are standard communication rules; TCP/IP is the foundational protocol stack of the Internet.
  • TCP/IP Layers: Application, Transport, Internet, and Network Access.
  • Domain Name System (DNS): Maps user-friendly text domain names directly to numeric IP destination addresses.
  • World Wide Web (WWW): An internet application using HTTP/HTTPS protocols to access linked web pages via URLs.
  • Telecommuting: Working remotely using internet and collaboration tools; increases flexibility but risks personal isolation and tracking difficulties.
